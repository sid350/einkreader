EinkReader privacy policy

Applies to the EinkReader app for iPhone, iPad and Mac (bundle identifier com.sid350.einkreader). Last updated: 19 November 2026.

Short version

Your books stay on your device. Syncing is optional and goes only through your own Google Drive. EinkReader does not collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and the developer has no server that receives your data.

Data stored on your device

The books you add, their covers, your reading positions, bookmarks and display settings are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. They are removed when you delete a book or the app. The app asks for no access to your photos, contacts, location, microphone or camera. Books are added through the system file picker or the Share and Open in commands, so the app reads only files you choose.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app uses your Google account only for syncing between your own devices:

  • It requests two permissions: access to the files the app itself creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file scope) and your e-mail address (to show which account is signed in).
  • In a folder it creates in your Drive it stores copies of the books you added and small files with your reading position and a random device identifier. The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on your device. Data goes directly between your device and Google; the developer never receives it and cannot read it.
  • You can disconnect the account in the app at any time, and you can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your books then stay on your device and in your Drive until you delete them.

Google handles this data under Google's privacy policy. EinkReader's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Network use

The only network connections the app makes are to Google, for the optional sync described above. Without signing in, the app works fully offline.

Children

The app is a general-purpose reader and is not directed at children; it collects no data from anyone.
